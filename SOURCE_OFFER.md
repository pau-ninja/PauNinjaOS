# Corresponding source offer

PauNinjaOS release manifests identify the exact downstream Git revision used to build each package. The complete downstream source, build scripts, locked inputs, and upstream source references are published with each release at https://github.com/pau-ninja/PauNinjaOS.

For GPL-covered binaries distributed in a PauNinjaOS release, Pau will provide the complete corresponding machine-readable source and the scripts used to control compilation and installation on request. This offer is valid for anyone who receives the binaries until at least 31 August 2029. Requests may be sent to hi@pau.me. Any physical-media charge will not exceed the reasonable cost of distribution.

Component copyright and license terms remain those of their respective upstream projects. This offer does not replace or narrow any right granted by those licenses.
